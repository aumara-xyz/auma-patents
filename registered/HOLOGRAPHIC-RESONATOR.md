## Summary (Public)

The present invention discloses a sovereign artificial intelligence architecture in which identity, memory, reasoning, voice, and governance are separated into distinct computational organs that communicate through governed interfaces rather than being conflated within a single model-weight space.

The Holographic Resonator integrates and governs the following organ subsystems: identity layer, memory layer (KIRA architecture), reasoning layer, voice and output layer, governance and safety layer (Paladin Protocol), and audit layer. Each organ operates independently and communicates through typed, validated message passing that enforces evidence classification and authorization at every interface.

The architecture addresses the technical gap in current large language model deployments where context, identity, memory, and safety behavior are intermixed, preventing independent verification, upgrade, or replacement of any individual cognitive function.

Key areas of novelty (details withheld): organ-to-organ governed interface protocol; holographic identity projection across organ boundaries; resonance synchronization for cross-organ state consistency; sovereign upgrade path enabling individual organ replacement without full system redeployment.
