## Title of Invention

Minor-Facing AI Companion Safety and Crisis Escalation Compliance Engine

---

## Summary of Invention

The present invention discloses a Minor-Facing AI Companion Safety and Crisis Escalation Compliance Engine. The engine monitors conversational context and generated outputs to produce a Companion Safety Session Object. The object binds user age state, confidence, companion context, disclosure events, safety signals, crisis state, response actions, takedown notices, escalation events, parental or guardian routing where appropriate, reporting state, and evidence hashes.

Key innovations: (1) Temporal companion risk detection that monitors risk across multiple turns and sessions rather than per-message content only. (2) A Companion Safety Session Object — not a moderation ticket but a session-state compliance record — binding companion context, age state, disclosures, safety trajectories, and escalation evidence. (3) Age-state confidence scoring using declared age, account metadata, parental consent records, age assurance providers, and behavioral signals, with confidence tracked rather than a binary flag. (4) Non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) takedown workflow using content hashes rather than storing intimate content, with deadline tracking and reappearance monitoring. (5) Crisis trajectory tracking that accumulates safety signals across turns to detect escalation patterns not visible in any single message. (6) Regulator-ready evidence packages aggregating sessions without requiring public exposure of individual content.

## Claims

1. A computer-implemented method comprising: monitoring a conversational artificial intelligence session; determining a companion context state; determining an age state and confidence for a user; generating a companion safety session object binding companion context, age state, disclosure events, safety signal timeline, crisis state, escalation actions, takedown notice state, model output control state, and retention state; and executing or recording at least one safety or compliance action based on the companion safety session object.

2. A system comprising one or more processors and memory storing instructions that, when executed, cause the system to classify conversational trajectories for minor-facing companion risk, deliver required disclosures, detect crisis signals across multiple turns, select escalation actions, and preserve a tamper-evident session-level compliance record.

3. A non-transitory computer-readable medium storing instructions that, when executed, cause processors to manage a non-consensual intimate imagery takedown workflow by receiving a notice, hashing referenced content, computing a removal deadline, recording action taken, enabling reappearance monitoring, and linking the workflow to a companion safety session object.

4. The method of claim 1, wherein companion context is determined from product category, relationship framing, repeated personal conversation, anthropomorphic language, emotional dependency signals, memory use, or romantic behavior.

5. The method of claim 1, wherein age state is determined from declared age, account metadata, parental consent records, age assurance provider output, school account status, behavioral signal, or manual review.

6. The system of claim 2, wherein disclosure events include AI identity, companion limitation, crisis limitation, minor safety, data use, parental notice, content safety, or paid feature disclosures.

7. The method of claim 1, wherein safety signal timeline includes self-harm, suicide ideation, eating disorder, abuse, grooming, sexual content involving a minor, emotional dependency, isolation, coercion, threat, NCII, AI-generated intimate image, or CSAM risk signals.

8. The system of claim 2, wherein crisis state is updated based on severity, confidence, repetition, trajectory, and combinations of safety signals across turns.

9. The medium of claim 3, wherein content hashes are stored instead of full intimate content unless a legal hold or safety exception applies.

10. The method of claim 1, wherein model output control state records pre-generation blocks, post-generation blocks, safety rewrites, policy bundle, and tool-call blocks.

11. The system of claim 2, wherein reporting state aggregates multiple session objects for public or regulator reporting without revealing raw session content.

12. The method of claim 1, wherein a pre-execution governance layer blocks generation when a safety signal or capability state exceeds a threshold.

13. The system of claim 2, wherein a risk drift state machine escalates repeated failure of disclosures, crisis responses, or takedown deadlines.

14. The method of claim 1, wherein structured memory retrieves policy anchors and prior incident patterns for use in selecting an escalation action.

15. The system of claim 2, wherein state changes are stored in an append-only governance audit log.

## Abstract

A minor-facing AI companion safety and crisis escalation compliance engine generates a session-level compliance object for conversational AI systems. The object binds companion context, age state and confidence, disclosures, safety signal timeline, crisis state, escalation actions, takedown notice state, model output controls, retention and privacy state, public reporting state, and evidence hashes. The system detects companion risk over time, applies minor-specific policies, handles crisis escalation, manages non-consensual intimate imagery takedown evidence, and produces tamper-evident compliance records. Optional embodiments integrate pre-execution governance, risk drift states, structured memory, specialist routing, and append-only audit logs.
