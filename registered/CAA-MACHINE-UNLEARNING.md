## Summary (Public)

The present invention discloses a regulatory-compliant machine unlearning system in which individual training data contributors can be attributed at the model parameter level, enabling surgical removal of a contributor's influence from deployed AI models upon erasure request.

The invention addresses the gap between current machine unlearning research (which offers approximate influence reduction) and regulatory requirements (GDPR Article 17, CCPA, HIPAA) for verifiable erasure, by providing cryptographic evidence of unlearning completion and residual influence bounds.

Key areas of novelty (details withheld): compartmentalized attribution architecture; contributor-aligned model partitioning; cryptographic erasure verification protocol; attribution-aware model lineage tracking across fine-tuning chains; multi-tenant erasure orchestration for shared model infrastructure.

Related filings: Per-Patient Surgical Erasure (AUMARA-D-PROV-001); Risk Drift Sentinel (AUMARA-RDS-PROV-001).
