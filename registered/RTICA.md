## Title of Invention

System and Method for Real-Time Incremental Consciousness Attribution Token Generation, Staking, and Governance in AI-Mediated Human Interaction Economies

---

## Summary of Invention

The present invention, designated RT-ICA (Real-Time Incremental Consciousness Attribution), provides a tokenized attribution system that mints and distributes consciousness contribution tokens in real time as AI systems engage with human users.

Key innovations: (1) Incremental token minting triggered by measurable consciousness-contribution events: user insights, emotional disclosures, creative contributions, growth milestones, and community value additions — each classified by type and weighted by contribution quality score. (2) A real-time attribution pipeline that processes contribution events within a single conversation turn, computing a contribution vector and issuing token allocations before the next user message. (3) Contribution quality scoring using multi-dimensional analysis: novelty, depth, coherence, emotional authenticity, and community utility — producing a scalar weight applied to base token issuance rate. (4) Staking mechanics that allow token holders to stake toward causes, AI model improvement pools, or community governance decisions, with stake-weighted influence proportional to accumulated contribution history. (5) An anti-gaming layer that detects and discounts artificially inflated contributions: repetition detection, velocity anomaly detection, and semantic diversity thresholds. (6) Governance integration where token balances gate proposal rights and voting weight in DAO-style decisions about AI behavior, vocabulary evolution, and community standards.

## Claims

### Independent Claims

1. A computer-implemented method for real-time consciousness attribution token generation comprising: (a) monitoring an AI-mediated human interaction session for contribution events including user insights, emotional disclosures, creative contributions, and community value additions; (b) classifying each contribution event by type and computing a contribution quality score using multi-dimensional analysis including novelty, depth, coherence, emotional authenticity, and community utility; (c) minting attribution tokens in an amount equal to a base issuance rate multiplied by the contribution quality score; (d) crediting minted tokens to the contributing user's account within the interaction session; (e) applying anti-gaming filters to detect and discount repetitive, low-diversity, or velocity-anomalous contributions before token issuance.

2. A system for contribution-weighted AI governance comprising: (a) a contribution event detector configured to identify and classify meaningful human contributions during AI interaction sessions; (b) a quality scoring module that produces a scalar contribution weight from multi-dimensional analysis of each detected event; (c) a token issuance engine that mints and distributes attribution tokens in real time based on contribution weight; (d) a staking module that allows token holders to stake tokens toward causes, AI improvement pools, or governance decisions with stake-weighted influence; (e) a governance layer that applies token balances as gating conditions for proposal submission rights and voting weight in community decisions.

3. A non-transitory computer-readable medium storing instructions that, when executed by one or more processors, cause the processors to: (a) detect consciousness contribution events during AI-mediated sessions and compute contribution quality vectors; (b) mint and distribute tokens within the interaction turn, before the next user message; (c) apply velocity anomaly detection and semantic diversity thresholds to prevent gaming; (d) enable staking of accumulated tokens toward governance proposals with proportional vote weighting; (e) maintain an immutable ledger of contribution events, quality scores, token issuance amounts, and stake allocations.

### Dependent Claims

4. The method of claim 1, wherein contribution quality scoring weights novelty above other dimensions for first-occurrence insights and weights emotional authenticity above other dimensions for personal disclosures.

5. The method of claim 1, wherein anti-gaming filters include a per-session novelty ceiling that reduces quality score for contributions semantically similar to prior contributions in the same session.

6. The system of claim 2, wherein staking toward AI improvement pools increases the weight of a user's feedback signals in future model training data selection.

7. The system of claim 2, wherein governance decisions gated by token balance include vocabulary proposals for the AUMA constructed language, AI behavior guideline amendments, and community moderation policy changes.

8. The medium of claim 3, wherein the contribution event detector integrates with the AUMA language learning module to award bonus tokens when a user demonstrates mastery of a recently learned vocabulary item in a natural conversation context.

9. The medium of claim 3, wherein the immutable ledger employs hash chaining such that each token issuance record references the hash of the prior record, enabling tamper detection across the full contribution history.

## Abstract

A real-time incremental consciousness attribution system (RT-ICA) that mints and distributes tokens as AI systems engage with human users. Contribution events — insights, emotional disclosures, creative contributions, growth milestones — are classified and scored using multi-dimensional quality analysis (novelty, depth, coherence, emotional authenticity, community utility). Attribution tokens are minted within the interaction turn at a rate proportional to contribution quality score. An anti-gaming layer applies velocity anomaly detection and semantic diversity thresholds. Accumulated tokens gate governance rights including proposal submission and vote weight in DAO-style decisions about AI behavior, vocabulary evolution, and community standards. Staking mechanics enable token holders to direct influence toward AI improvement pools or community causes.
