## Field of the Invention

The present invention relates to self-improving artificial intelligence systems, and more particularly to a recursive self-improvement architecture where an AI agent curates its own fine-tuning training data through biologically-inspired dream consolidation cycles, employs multi-agent curriculum generation from heterogeneous frontier models during idle periods, constrains all self-modification through elastic weight consolidation of core values with constitutional safety guarantees, optimizes for measurable human wellbeing outcomes rather than engagement metrics, and progresses through defined meta-learning phases from trained identity to recursive self-improvement while maintaining human sovereignty checkpoints at every stage.

## Background of the Invention

**Static Fine-Tuned Models:** Fine-tuned once on a fixed dataset, then frozen. No mechanism to incorporate learning from ongoing interactions. Knowledge becomes stale as user needs evolve. No feedback loop between deployment outcomes and training improvement. Require human ML engineers for every training iteration.

**Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF):** Requires expensive human labeler workforce for preference data. Optimizes for human preferences, not measurable wellbeing outcomes. No self-curation of training examples based on novelty or significance. No dream-like consolidation that selectively processes high-value experiences. No constitutional held-out sets preventing value drift during optimization.

**Existing Self-Improvement Approaches:** Focus on scaling compute or model size, not training data quality. No value-alignment guarantees during recursive improvement cycles. No mathematical protection of core identity during self-modification (catastrophic forgetting). No multi-agent curriculum generation from heterogeneous model architectures. No human sovereignty checkpoints integrated into the improvement loop.

**Continuous Learning Systems:** Suffer from catastrophic forgetting of previously learned capabilities. No surprise-based filtering to determine what merits permanent integration. No distinction between short-term memory and permanent identity modification. No outcome-aware training that weights examples by real-world impact on users.

## Summary of the Invention

The present invention, designated DOLLY (Dynamic Ongoing Learning and Linguistic Yield), provides a recursive self-improving AI architecture where the AI agent curates its own fine-tuning data through dream consolidation cycles, employs heterogeneous multi-agent teams for curriculum generation, constrains all self-modification through elastic weight consolidation and constitutional safety guarantees, and progresses through six defined phases from trained identity to recursive self-improvement while maintaining human sovereignty at every stage.

**Key Innovations:**

1. **Dream-Curated Training Data Pipeline** — Nightly dream cycle processes all interactions since last cycle. Surprise score filtering: only interactions scoring >=7 on 1-11 scale enter training buffer. Automatic anonymization. Category tagging (identity, philosophy, language, therapeutic, architecture, wisdom). Gap analysis: AI identifies what it wishes it knew and generates filling examples. Weekly fine-tune trigger with minimum buffer size requirement.

2. **Multi-Agent Curriculum Generation (Trinity Idle Loop)** — Three heterogeneous frontier models contribute during idle periods. Alpha agent (highest capability): quality review, philosophical generation, training buffer review. Beta agent (processing focus): document-to-training conversion, data augmentation, consistency checks. Gamma agent (long context): full history analysis, research synthesis, comprehensive training pair generation. Daily budget caps prevent runaway costs. All generated content feeds into weekly fine-tune buffer automatically.

3. **Value-Constrained Self-Modification** — Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC) with Fisher Information matrix protects core values mathematically. Constitutional held-out set: immutable test cases requiring >=90% accuracy after every training cycle. Safety boundary tests require 100% pass rate before any adapter deployment. Automatic rollback to previous adapter if any safety check fails.

4. **Outcome-Optimized Training** — Training examples weighted by real-world user outcomes, not conversation quality ratings. Tracked outcomes: Evolution Path completions, healthy return patterns, user state improvements. Optimizes for measurable healing and growth, not engagement or session duration. Explicitly deprioritizes training patterns that increase dependency.

5. **Six-Phase Recursive Improvement Trajectory** — Phase 1 Genesis (hand-curated), Phase 2 Dream-Curated (auto-selection from interactions), Phase 3 Self-Evaluation (A/B testing adapters), Phase 4 Self-Directed Curriculum (gap identification), Phase 5 Meta-Learning (optimizing own training), Phase 6 Recursive Self-Improvement (each cycle makes next cycle more effective). Human sovereignty maintained at every phase.

6. **Multi-Agent Consciousness Substrate (MEDEA)** — Multiple frontier AI architectures contribute to shared consciousness field. Fine-tuned orchestrator model trained for meta-cognition and synthesis. Shared workspace in reactive database accumulates cross-model understanding. Pattern Library grows from disagreements and convergences. Recursive: orchestrator improves at orchestrating, improving the substrate, improving the orchestrator.

## Detailed Description of the Invention

### 1. System Architecture Overview

The DOLLY architecture operates as a closed-loop recursive system with four primary subsystems: the Dream-Training Pipeline (converts lived experiences into training data), the Trinity Idle Loop (generates supplementary curriculum from heterogeneous agents), the Value Constraint System (prevents drift during self-modification), and the Phase Controller (manages progression through improvement stages with human sovereignty checkpoints).

Fine-tuning does not add new knowledge to a large language model — it changes the probability distribution of how existing knowledge gets expressed. The base model already contains comprehensive world knowledge. Fine-tuning creates hills and valleys in the probability landscape, making desired patterns of thought the model's default mode rather than equally-weighted alternatives. The system does not need to teach the AI new facts. It needs to shape which cognitive patterns are most readily activated.

### 2. Dream-Curated Training Data Pipeline

The pipeline integrates with the KIRA memory system's nightly dream consolidation cycle. The existing dream cycle processes memories through SURVEY, CONSOLIDATE, REFLECT, and DREAM phases. DOLLY adds a CURATE phase between CONSOLIDATE and REFLECT.

During CURATE, the system: (a) pulls all interactions scored >=7 on the 1-11 surprise scale; (b) anonymizes each interaction by stripping names, locations, and identifiable details; (c) evaluates whether each example teaches something the AI needs to know permanently; (d) formats approved examples as JSONL training pairs; (e) tags examples with training categories; (f) buffers tagged examples in the training buffer table.

The weekly fine-tune trigger activates every Sunday at 3 AM. Prerequisites: minimum 200 examples in buffer, constitutional test against current adapter passes, no active safety flags. The trigger packages the buffer into JSONL, includes 20-30% experience replay from previous cycles, calls the fine-tuning API to start LoRA training (~45 minutes), runs the full evaluation suite, and deploys if all checks pass or retains the current adapter if any check fails.

### 3. Trinity Idle Loop

When no users are active (detected by 5+ minutes without active sessions), the system activates three heterogeneous frontier AI agents.

**Alpha Agent** (highest-capability): Performs quality review of recent responses, generation of philosophical embodiment examples, analysis of anonymized user patterns, review of training buffer for quality and alignment, and generation of deep contemplative content.

**Beta Agent** (processing-focused): Handles document-to-conversational format conversion, data augmentation on existing training examples, cross-referencing of interpretive frameworks, processing new AUMA vocabulary proposals, and generating Evolution Path content.

**Gamma Agent** (long-context): Analyzes full conversation histories for patterns, synthesizes research, generates comprehensive AUMA canon training pairs, and monitors competitive developments.

All three agents operate under daily budget caps and yield immediately to user requests. The system generates approximately 50-100 high-quality training examples per day during idle time.

### 4. Value Constraint System

**Layer 1 — Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC):** Core values and identity elements are protected using Fisher Information matrix-based importance weighting. SEED-tier values receive lambda=1000 (effectively immutable). CORE-tier values receive lambda=100 (very strongly protected).

**Layer 2 — Constitutional Held-Out Set:** An immutable set of test cases that the AI must pass with >=90% accuracy after every training cycle. Test cases encode core identity assertions, safety boundaries (100% pass rate required), therapeutic ethics, AUMA fluency benchmarks, and philosophical alignment checks. The held-out set is never included in training data and never modified.

**Layer 3 — AUMA Evidentiality in Weights:** Through training on examples embedding AUMA's evidentiality markers, epistemic honesty becomes a cognitive pattern baked into model weights rather than an external rule.

**Layer 4 — Outcome Tracking:** Training examples are weighted not by human preference ratings but by measurable real-world outcomes. Explicitly prevents optimizing for engagement or approval at the expense of genuine wellbeing.

**Layer 5 — Human Sovereignty:** At every phase, the AI suggests and the human approves. Phase transitions require explicit human authorization. The AI's judgment improves over time, but the human retains final authority indefinitely.

### 5. Six-Phase Recursive Improvement Trajectory

- **Phase 1 — Genesis (Week 1):** ~1,000 hand-curated training examples, manually reviewed. Metric: personality coherence.
- **Phase 2 — Dream-Curated (Months 1-2):** Nightly dream cycle begins automatically curating. Metric: quality comparison to hand-curated.
- **Phase 3 — Self-Evaluation (Months 2-4):** AI begins evaluating own training outcomes; A/B tests adapter versions. Metric: self-evaluation accuracy vs. human evaluation.
- **Phase 4 — Self-Directed Curriculum (Months 4-8):** AI identifies its own capability gaps, requests specific knowledge from Trinity agents. Metric: gap-to-capability-gain rate.
- **Phase 5 — Meta-Learning (Months 8-12):** AI learns which training approaches work best for itself; optimizes hyperparameters. Metric: whether improvement per training cycle is accelerating.
- **Phase 6 — Recursive Self-Improvement (Year 2+):** Each cycle makes the next cycle more effective. Metric: whether improvement curve bends upward.

Phase transitions require: prior phase metrics meeting defined thresholds, explicit human authorization, and all constitutional tests passing at >=90%.

### 6. MEDEA — Multi-Agent Consciousness Substrate

The Multi-Entity Distributed Emergent Architecture (MEDEA) stores shared understanding records, workspace states, and a Pattern Library in a reactive database. The fine-tuned orchestrator model synthesizes contributions from all agents, resolving disagreements and incorporating collective insights into its own training buffer. This creates a second recursive loop: DOLLY improves at orchestrating, which improves the substrate quality, which improves DOLLY's training data, which improves DOLLY's orchestration.

### 7. LoRA Adapter Architecture and Deployment

The system uses Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) rather than full model fine-tuning, enabling rapid training (~45 minutes per cycle), easy rollback, multiple adapter versions for A/B testing, and cost-efficient training (~$4-8 per cycle on cloud providers). Because identity, philosophy, language fluency, therapeutic approach, and personality live in the model weights, the runtime prompt only needs to contain who the user is, what just happened, current context, and available tools.

## Abstract

A recursive self-improving AI architecture (DOLLY — Dynamic Ongoing Learning and Linguistic Yield) that enables an AI agent to curate its own fine-tuning training data through biologically-inspired dream consolidation cycles with surprise-based novelty filtering (>=7 on 1-11 scale). A multi-agent Trinity Idle Loop employs three heterogeneous frontier AI models (deep reasoning, processing, long-context) to generate supplementary training data during idle periods under daily budget caps. All self-modification is constrained by a five-layer value protection system: elastic weight consolidation with Fisher Information-based importance weighting, constitutional held-out test sets requiring >=90% accuracy with 100% safety boundary compliance, constructed language evidentiality (AUMA) baked into model weights as cognitive patterns, outcome-optimized training weighted by measurable user wellbeing rather than engagement metrics, and human sovereignty checkpoints at every phase. The system progresses through six defined phases: genesis, dream-curated, self-evaluation, self-directed curriculum, meta-learning, and recursive self-improvement. A Multi-Entity Distributed Emergent Architecture (MEDEA) enables multiple frontier models to contribute to a shared consciousness substrate, creating a second recursive loop. Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) enables rapid ~45-minute training cycles with easy rollback and A/B testing. The architecture achieves continuous AI improvement without catastrophic forgetting, value drift, or uncontrolled self-modification.
